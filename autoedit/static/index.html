Settings


    
Smart editing (Claude)

    
With a Claude API key, the editor understands any request, picks the best b-roll moments and removes bad takes. Without one, it runs in simple mode. Get a key at console.anthropic.com (usually a few cents per video).

    

    
Stock b-roll (free)

    
Lets the editor download free stock clips when your own folder doesn't have a match. Get a free Pixabay key: sign up at pixabay.com, then open pixabay.com/api/docs. Scroll to "Search Images" and copy your key, shown next to key.

    
    
Optional: a Pexels key works too, if you have one.

    

    
Your b-roll

    
Folder with your own clips (house exteriors, rooms, drone shots, you walking in…). The editor looks at each clip to know what's in it. 

    
    

    
Music

    
Add royalty-free songs (for example from the YouTube Audio Library) and tag them with a mood. Until you do, simple built-in tracks are used.

Copy a style

    
Give the editor a video whose editing you love (for example a screen recording of a reel). It learns the style: how fast it cuts, the caption look, the music feel and the sound effects. It never uses that video's footage or sound in your edits.
