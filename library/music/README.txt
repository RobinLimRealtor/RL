Put royalty-free songs here (mp3, wav, m4a).

Sort them into folders named by mood so the editor can pick the right one:
  upbeat, calm, inspiring, luxury, dramatic, fun

Example:  library/music/upbeat/sunny-day.mp3

Good free sources: YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com > Audio Library),
Pixabay Music. Until you add songs, simple built-in tracks are used.
You can also add songs from the app: Settings > Music > Add songs.
